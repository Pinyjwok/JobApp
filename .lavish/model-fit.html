Model fit, per agent

Each of your 20 agents mapped to a task class, then three model picks — a cost floor, a middle-ground default, a quality ceiling — plus a suggested reasoning level. Picks spread across Anthropic, Google, OpenAI and DeepSeek by task shape, not brand.

The three tiers

1

Cost

Cheapest model that still clears the bar. For mechanical, high-volume, judgment-light nodes. Never where a hallucination ships into the CV.

Flash-Lite · DeepSeek V3.2 · Haiku 4.5

2

Middle ★

The sane default. ~80% of frontier quality at a fraction of cost; caching closes the rest. Most nodes belong here.

Sonnet 4.6 · Gemini 3.1 Pro · GPT-5.4

3

Quality

Frontier reasoning or best-in-class prose. Reserve for irreducible judgment and the writing users actually read.

Opus 4.7 · Fable 5 (prose) · Gemini 3.1 Pro

Reasoning levels

How much thinking budget to allocate (reasoning-effort on reasoning-capable models; for non-reasoning models, read as "task depth"). More reasoning = slower + pricier, so match it to the task, don't max it everywhere.

None / Minimal

Map, format, dispatch. No chain-of-thought — it only adds latency + cost.

Low

Parse ambiguity, classify, hold a conversation. A little planning.

Medium

Synthesis + craft: research framing, prose over facts, editorial polish.

High

Irreducible judgment: fit/gap calls, semantic entailment, fabrication hunts.

Quick wins — cut cost, ~zero risk

  • Credentials Formatter: Flash 3.0 → Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite, reasoning off. Pure field-map.
  • MO + Assembly Coordinator: → Haiku 4.5, low reasoning. They dispatch, not reason.
  • Extractor / ProjectSetup: Gemini 2.5 Flash at 90%+, 1/20th Sonnet's output price.
  • Analyst Validator: DeepSeek R4 Pro already right; probe V3.2 for more savings.

Don't cheap out

  • Integrity Checker: fabrication gate, high reasoning. A miss ships a lie. Sonnet floor, Opus for hard ones.
  • Cover Letter + Style Reviewer: what the user reads. Sonnet min; Fable 5 tops EQ-Bench prose.
  • Profile Builder / History Formatter: fact-bearing — temp ≤ 1.0, stay Sonnet+. Cheap models fabricate to fill word floors.
  • Analyst: downstream trusts its gap calls. High reasoning; V3.2 only if you accept edge-case noise.

Constraints baked into your stack

  • KEMU routes via OpenRouter — picks must be OpenRouter-served. All four providers are.
  • max_tokens hardcoded 65536; OpenRouter key cap must exceed it. Don't pick a lower-cap model.
  • Temp 0–2. Gate/format → 0. Fact-writers → ≤1.0. Never >1.0 on fact-bearing output (your rule).
  • Caching drops Sonnet effective input to ~$0.30/M at 60%+ hit — middle-ground beats "cheap" once cached.
  • Reasoning effort costs tokens + latency — only the High-tier judgment nodes justify it; keep it off for mechanical/routing.
