Ready to Accelerate Your HR Career?

Are you passionate about helping small businesses thrive and looking to dive into the world of HR? Join us at The Save Group, where every day brings new, opportunities to learn and grow, and the perk of Birthday Leave and 4pm Fridays!


Who We Are:

We're not just another HR provider - we're the go-to experts for small businesses in need of smart, creative, and proactive people solutions. With a focus on companies with fewer than 50 employees, our close-knit team brings unique products and client-focused solutions to a variety of industries. We're a dynamic team that's all about professional growth, collaboration, and making a real impact.


What You'll Tackle Daily:

As our Junior People & Culture Consultant, you’ll step into a hands-on, varied role designed to fast‑track your career in HR consulting. From day one, you’ll work alongside experienced Lead Consultants, gaining real exposure to client-facing work and building the practical skills needed to become a trusted HR advisor.

This role offers a clear and supported growth pathway, from assisting Lead Consultants, to managing your own clients as your capability and confidence develop. We’re committed to developing you into a well-rounded, commercially savvy HR consultant, where your remuneration will grow with you (ability to earn un-capped commission in the future)!

Here’s a taste of what you’ll be doing:

Drafting and preparing essential HR documents like employment contracts, letters, and policies.

Providing basic HR advice and interpreting Modern Awards under the guidance of our Lead Consultants.

Assisting with client HR Audits to assess compliance and identify business improvements.

Supporting performance management, employee relations, terminations, and redundancies.

Supporting the full recruitment process, from writing job ads to onboarding new hires.

Helping with proactive HR management solutions, including training sessions, management coaching, and employee engagement or alignment surveys.

Juggling multiple clients across varying industries and learning the ropes of HR consulting in a fast-paced environment.

Gradually building and managing your own client portfolio with ongoing support and mentorship.


Your Essential Skillset:

HR degree, certificate, or diploma. Ideal, but not mandatory (we prefer experience)!

Got at least a year of HR experience, especially in B2B or consulting? Perfect!

Know the basics of Aussie employment law, NES, and Modern Awards? Awesome!

Can you make sense of HR docs and dig into legislation? You’re the one!

Super motivated and ready to learn and grow? We love that!

Comfortable chatting with business owners and have strong communication skills? Yes, please!

Detail-oriented, organized, and can juggle time like a pro? We need you!

Can handle multiple clients and industries without breaking a sweat? Excellent!

Great at solving problems and navigating challenges? That’s what we’re looking for!

Proficient with MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)? You’ve got the tools!

Must have Australian work rights to be eligible for this position.


Why You'll Love Working with Us:

At The Save Group, we're not just offering a job, we're offering a place where you can grow, learn, and make a difference. Here's what makes us special:

You'll gain valuable business acumen, including how to manage your daily billable hour targets to thrive and earn bonuses as a HR consultant.

Deal with dominant business owners, learning how to navigate and influence in a client-first environment.

Multi-task across all facets of HR, ensuring no two days are the same, and every experience adds to your professional toolkit.

Start small and grow big! With Lead Consultant oversight, you'll gradually build and manage your own client portfolio, gaining independence and expertise along the way (no cold selling or client hunting required).

We invest in your development with both internal and external training. You'll have the chance to expand your skills and advance your career in HR. Clear career progression through our TSG learning academy.

Enjoy a competitive salary package, monthly bonus incentives, access to salary increases for high performers, birthday leave, and of course 4pm Fridays!
