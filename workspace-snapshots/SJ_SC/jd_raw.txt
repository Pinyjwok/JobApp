ABOUT MELBOURNE PATHOLOGY

Melbourne Pathology is one of Victoria's leading providers of pathology services, delivering high-quality diagnostic testing to clinicians and patients across metropolitan Melbourne and regional Victoria. As part of Australian Clinical Labs (ASX: ACL), we operate a network of collection centres and laboratories staffed by experienced scientists, technicians, and pathologists. Our Heidelberg laboratory is a high-throughput facility processing over 15,000 specimens daily across multiple disciplines.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Laboratory Technician (Grade 1) to join our Biochemistry department at the Heidelberg laboratory. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate to gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced clinical pathology environment. The successful candidate will work as part of a collaborative team performing routine and specialised biochemical analyses on patient specimens.

You will receive comprehensive on-the-job training and mentoring from our experienced team of scientists and senior technicians. This role offers a genuine pathway for career development within one of Australia's largest pathology networks.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Specimen Reception and Processing
- Receive, sort, and process patient specimens (blood, urine, and other body fluids) in accordance with standard operating procedures (SOPs)
- Verify specimen identification, labelling, and integrity prior to analysis
- Centrifuge, aliquot, and prepare specimens for testing on automated platforms
- Identify and manage unsuitable or incorrectly labelled specimens per rejection criteria

Analytical Testing
- Operate and perform routine maintenance on automated chemistry analysers, including Roche cobas 8000 modular analyser series and Abbott Architect ci16200
- Perform routine biochemistry testing including electrolytes, liver function tests (LFTs), renal function, lipid profiles, glucose, cardiac markers, thyroid function, and tumour markers
- Process urgent (STAT) specimens within required turnaround times
- Perform manual testing procedures as required, including urine chemistry and body fluid analysis

Quality Assurance and Quality Control
- Perform internal quality control (IQC) procedures at the beginning of each shift and at designated intervals throughout the day
- Review QC results against Westgard rules and escalate out-of-range results to the Senior Scientist or Laboratory Manager
- Participate in External Quality Assurance (EQA) programs, including RCPA Quality Assurance Programs (RCPAQAP)
- Document all QC failures, corrective actions, and instrument flags in accordance with laboratory policy
- Assist with calibration verification and linearity checks as directed

Data Management and Reporting
- Enter, verify, and validate patient results in the Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)
- Ensure accurate transcription and data entry of results for manual tests
- Identify and flag critical results for immediate notification to requesting clinicians in line with the critical results policy
- Assist with resolution of queries from clinicians and collection centre staff

Laboratory Safety and Compliance
- Adhere to all workplace health and safety (WHS) policies and procedures
- Follow infection control and biohazard handling protocols at all times
- Maintain a clean, organised, and safe working environment
- Participate in laboratory safety audits and risk assessments as required
- Comply with NATA (National Association of Testing Authorities) accreditation requirements and the National Pathology Accreditation Advisory Council (NPAAC) standards
- Handle and dispose of biological and chemical waste in accordance with EPA Victoria regulations

General Duties
- Participate in laboratory meetings, training sessions, and continuing professional development (CPD) activities
- Assist with stock management, reagent preparation, and inventory control
- Contribute to the review and updating of SOPs and work instructions
- Support other laboratory sections during periods of high workload as directed
- Participate in the after-hours on-call roster as required (with appropriate penalty rates)
